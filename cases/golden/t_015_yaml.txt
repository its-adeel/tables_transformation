Table 2 "R-R" Rural Residential District Site and Building Development Regulations:
  Site Planning:
    Minimum lot area: 3 acres
    Maximum lot configuration: '3:1 ratio — lot depth to width (Note: 1)'
    Minimum lot dimensions:
      Width: 200 feet
      Depth: 600 feet
    Parking: '4 parking spaces (Note: 2)'
  Building:
    Dwelling unit size:
      Single story units: 1,100 square feet
      1½ and 2 story units: 1,300 square feet
    Minimum setbacks:
      Front: 60 feet
      Interior side yard: 50 feet
      Rear yard: 100 feet
    Maximum building height: 35 feet
