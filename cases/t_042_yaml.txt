Use Categories:
  1. RESIDENTIAL:
    A. Household living:
      Specific Uses > 1. Residence above first floor:
        Minimum Number of Vehicle Spaces: 1 per residence + business parking
      Specific Uses > 2. Bed and breakfast:
        Minimum Number of Vehicle Spaces: 1 per guest room + 2 spaces for owner's portion
      Specific Uses > 3. Rooming/board house:
        Minimum Number of Vehicle Spaces: 1 per rooming unit
      Specific Uses > 4. Dormitories, Fraternities, Sororities:
        Minimum Number of Vehicle Spaces: 1 per 2 residents
      Specific Uses > 5. Single-family and duplex, triplex, four-plex:
        Minimum Number of Vehicle Spaces: 2 per dwelling unit
      Specific Uses > 6. Elderly apartment complex:
        Minimum Number of Vehicle Spaces: 0.75 per dwelling unit
      Specific Uses > 7. Multi-family, 5 units or more:
        Minimum Number of Vehicle Spaces: 1.5 per unit
      Specific Uses > 8. Studio Apartments:
        Minimum Number of Vehicle Spaces: 1 per unit
      Specific Uses > 9. All other household living:
        Minimum Number of Vehicle Spaces: 1 per unit
    B. Group living:
      Specific Uses > 1. Nursing homes; assisted living facility; in-patient substance abuse treatment facility; group living facilities; res. care facility for elderly:
        Minimum Number of Vehicle Spaces: 1 per 2.5 beds
      Specific Uses > 2. Outpatient substance abuse treatment facility:
        Minimum Number of Vehicle Spaces: 1 per 200 sf GFA
      Specific Uses > 3. Other group living:
        Minimum Number of Vehicle Spaces: 1 per 4 beds
  2. INSTITUTIONAL AND CIVIC:
    A. Schools:
      Specific Uses > 1. Elementary and junior highs:
        Minimum Number of Vehicle Spaces: 2 per classroom plus 1 for each 5 seats of the largest place of public assembly (stadium, auditorium or gym) whichever requires the greatest number of parking spaces.
      Specific Uses > 2. High schools:
        Minimum Number of Vehicle Spaces: 1 per 4 students plus 1 for each 5 seats of the largest place of public assembly (stadium, auditorium or gym) whichever requires the greatest number of parking spaces.
      Specific Uses > 3. Schools of private instruction:
        Minimum Number of Vehicle Spaces: 1 per 200 sf GFA
      Specific Uses > 4. College, vocational/technical schools:
        Minimum Number of Vehicle Spaces: 1 per 200 sf GFA
    B. Community services:
      Specific Uses: 1. Community center
      Minimum Number of Vehicle Spaces: 1 per 200 sf GFA
    C. Cultural:
      Specific Uses: 1. Museums, art galleries & libraries
      Minimum Number of Vehicle Spaces: 1 per 1,000 sf GFA
    D. Day care:
      Specific Uses: 1. Day care, pre-school & similar uses
      Minimum Number of Vehicle Spaces: 1 per employee and staff member plus 1 for each 5 children
    E. Detention facilities:
      Specific Uses: 1. Jails, honor camps, reformatories, law enforcement rehabilitation centers
      Minimum Number of Vehicle Spaces: 1 per employee on maximum shift, plus 1 per service vehicle
    F. Hospital/clinic:
      Specific Uses > 1. Hospital:
        Minimum Number of Vehicle Spaces: 2.5 per bed for in-patient care facilities plus 1 per each 250 sq. ft. of floor area for outpatient facilities
      Specific Uses > 2. Clinic:
        Minimum Number of Vehicle Spaces: 1 per 200 sf
    G. Laboratories & research:
      Specific Uses: 1. Laboratories and research facilities
      Minimum Number of Vehicle Spaces: 1 per 300 sf
    H. Parks and open areas:
      Specific Uses > 1. Golf Course:
        Minimum Number of Vehicle Spaces: 6 spaces per hole
      Specific Uses > 2. All other:
        Minimum Number of Vehicle Spaces: 20 spaces per athletic field or ball diamond or 1 per 4 seats whichever results in more spaces
    I. Private clubs, lodges:
      Specific Uses: 1. Private clubs, country clubs, lodges
      Minimum Number of Vehicle Spaces: 1 per 4 persons based on maximum occupancy
    J. Radio/television:
      Specific Uses: 1. Broadcast stations
      Minimum Number of Vehicle Spaces: 1 per 300 sf GFA
    K. Religious assembly:
      Specific Uses: 1. Churches, synagogues, temple, etc.
      Minimum Number of Vehicle Spaces: 1 per 4 persons based on maximum occupancy in auditorium
    L. Safety services:
      Specific Uses: 1. Police, fire, emergency response
      Minimum Number of Vehicle Spaces: 1 per 200 sf
  3. COMMERCIAL:
    A. Recreation and entertainment:
      Specific Uses > 1. Assembly/auditorium/theater:
        Minimum Number of Vehicle Spaces: 1 per 4 persons based on maximum occupancy or 1 per 50 sf if seats are not permanent
      Specific Uses > 2. Amusement center, park:
        Minimum Number of Vehicle Spaces: 1 per 5,000 sf of gross land area or 1 for each 4 persons based on maximum occupancy
      Specific Uses > 3. Bowling alley:
        Minimum Number of Vehicle Spaces: 4 per lane
      Specific Uses > 4. Clubs/lodges, casino, guest ranch, resort cabins and lodges:
        Minimum Number of Vehicle Spaces: 1 per 3 persons based on maximum occupancy
      Specific Uses > 5. Health club/fitness center:
        Minimum Number of Vehicle Spaces: 1 per 200 sf
      Specific Uses > 6. Marina/yacht club:
        Minimum Number of Vehicle Spaces: 1 per 3 boat slips
      Specific Uses > 7. Miniature golf:
        Minimum Number of Vehicle Spaces: 2 per hole
      Specific Uses > 8. RV park or campground:
        Minimum Number of Vehicle Spaces: 1 per camping space plus 4 for office
    B. Community service:
      Specific Uses > 1. Community activity building:
        Minimum Number of Vehicle Spaces: 1 per 500 sf GFA
      Specific Uses > 2. All other community service and philanthropic:
        Minimum Number of Vehicle Spaces: 1 per 500 sf GFA
    C. Office:
      Specific Uses > 1. General and governmental offices, in all districts except C-3:
        Minimum Number of Vehicle Spaces: 1 per 300 sf GFA
      Specific Uses > 2. General and governmental offices in the C-3 District:
        Minimum Number of Vehicle Spaces: 1 per 500 sf GFA
      Specific Uses > 3. Medical, dental, counseling:
        Minimum Number of Vehicle Spaces: 1 per 200 sf GFA
    D. Parking, commercial:
      Specific Uses: 1. N/A
      Minimum Number of Vehicle Spaces: N/A
    E. Recreation and entertainment, outdoor:
      Specific Uses > 1. Driving range:
        Minimum Number of Vehicle Spaces: 1 per tee
      Specific Uses > 2. Batting cage, waterslide, swim pool:
        Minimum Number of Vehicle Spaces: 2 per cage; other - per site plan review based on maximum occupancy
      Specific Uses > 3. Miniature golf:
        Minimum Number of Vehicle Spaces: 2 per hole
      Specific Uses > 4. All other outdoor recreation:
        Minimum Number of Vehicle Spaces: Per site plan review based on maximum occupancy
    F. Drive-in, drive-thru uses:
      Specific Uses > 1. Automated teller machines:
        Minimum Number of Vehicle Spaces: 4 per ATM lane
      Specific Uses > 2. Drive-thru cleaners:
        Minimum Number of Vehicle Spaces: 4 per window
      Specific Uses > 3. Drive-thru liquor:
        Minimum Number of Vehicle Spaces: 3 per window
      Specific Uses > 4. Restaurant, drive-in, no indoor seating:
        Minimum Number of Vehicle Spaces: 1 parking space per employee on maximum shift
      Specific Uses > 5. Restaurant, fast-food with drive-thru facilities:
        Minimum Number of Vehicle Spaces: 1 per 100 sf gross floor area including outdoor seating area
    G. Retail sales and services:
      Specific Uses > 1. Funeral home, mortuary, crematorium:
        Minimum Number of Vehicle Spaces: 1 per 4 seats + 1 per employee
      Specific Uses > 2. Restaurants, no drive through:
        Minimum Number of Vehicle Spaces: 1 per 100 sf gross floor area including outdoor seating area
      Specific Uses > 3. Shopping centers:
        Minimum Number of Vehicle Spaces: 1 per 250 sf
      Specific Uses > 4. General retail:
        Minimum Number of Vehicle Spaces: 1 per 250 sf
      Specific Uses > 5. Furniture stores and other low volume retail:
        Minimum Number of Vehicle Spaces: 1 per 500 sf
      Specific Uses > 6. Bar or tavern:
        Minimum Number of Vehicle Spaces: 1 per 100 sf gross floor area including outdoor seating area
      Specific Uses > 7. Bank:
        Minimum Number of Vehicle Spaces: 1 per 300 sf
      Specific Uses > 8. Event center or banquet hall:
        Minimum Number of Vehicle Spaces: 1 space per 200 sf GFA
      Specific Uses > 9. Nightclub:
        Minimum Number of Vehicle Spaces: 1 space per 100 sf GFA
      Specific Uses > 10. After hours establishment:
        Minimum Number of Vehicle Spaces: 1 space per 100 sf GFA
      Specific Uses > 11. Smoking lounge:
        Minimum Number of Vehicle Spaces: 1 space per 100 sf GFA
    H. Self-service storage:
      Specific Uses: 1. Mini-warehouse
      Minimum Number of Vehicle Spaces: 1 per 8 storage units
    I. Vehicle service, limited:
      Specific Uses > 1. Auto service, repair, body shop:
        Minimum Number of Vehicle Spaces: 2 per service bay + 1 per employee
      Specific Uses > 2. Car wash, self-service:
        Minimum Number of Vehicle Spaces: 2 per stall
      Specific Uses > 3. Car wash, full-service:
        Minimum Number of Vehicle Spaces: 6 per stall
      Specific Uses > 4. Gasoline station:
        Minimum Number of Vehicle Spaces: 1.5 per fuel nozzle plus 1 per 200 square feet of store
      Specific Uses > 5. Oil, lube, muffler:
        Minimum Number of Vehicle Spaces: 4 per service bay + 1 per employee
      Specific Uses > 6. Tire, batteries, accessory retailers:
        Minimum Number of Vehicle Spaces: 1 per 300 sf GFA
    J. Vehicle retail:
      Specific Uses > 1. Vehicle sales, new & used, including recreational vehicles/boats:
        Minimum Number of Vehicle Spaces: Spaces equal to 10 percent of vehicle display area
      Specific Uses > 2. Large truck, equipment sales, boat sales:
        Minimum Number of Vehicle Spaces: 1 per 1,000 sf
    K. Lodging:
      Specific Uses: 1. Hotel or motel
      Minimum Number of Vehicle Spaces: 1 per room plus 75% of the required parking for attached uses
  4. INDUSTRIAL:
    A. Industrial service and manufacturing/production:
      Specific Uses > 1. Indoor and outdoor operations:
        Minimum Number of Vehicle Spaces: 1.1 per employee
      Specific Uses > 2. Bottling works, creamery, lumberyard:
        Minimum Number of Vehicle Spaces: 2 per 3 employee + 1 per service vehicle
    B. Warehouse and freight movement:
      Specific Uses: 1. Warehouse, freight movement facilities
      Minimum Number of Vehicle Spaces: 1 per 5,000 sf; 5 spaces minimum
    C. Waste-related use:
      Specific Uses: 1. Landfill/solid waste facility
      Minimum Number of Vehicle Spaces: 1.1 per employee
    D. Wholesale sales:
      Specific Uses: 1. Warehousing, equipment servicing & manufacturing area
      Minimum Number of Vehicle Spaces: 1.1 per employee
